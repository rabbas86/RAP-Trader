# Phase 6: Technical Analyst

Phase 6 completes strict technical models, price/volatility/volume indicators, causally confirmed market structure, clustered support and resistance, structured evidence, confidence-capped synthesis, provenance, API and CLI interfaces, and safety tests.

No network, LLM, model download, broker, execution, risk engine, portfolio, committee, Chairman, or live-trading path is introduced. Kronos forecasts and Phase 4 backtests are optional research evidence only. Outputs remain `BULLISH`, `BEARISH`, `NEUTRAL`, `MIXED`, or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

Bars after `as_of` are discarded, pivots require two later bars for confirmation, and structure and levels consume confirmed swings only.
